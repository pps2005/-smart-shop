Privacy Policy — Xiress Studio

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Xiress Studio is a personal content studio used by its owner to create original AI-made short videos and publish them to the owner's own TikTok account.

What we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, we receive an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID from TikTok. We use them only to upload videos created in Xiress Studio to that same account, at the owner's request.

What we store

Tokens are stored privately on our server and are never sold, shared or used for advertising. We do not collect personal data from viewers or other TikTok users.

Removal

The owner can disconnect at any time from the studio, or revoke access in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

For questions, contact the studio owner through the TikTok account that publishes Xiress Studio videos.
